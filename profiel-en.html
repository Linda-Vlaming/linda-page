Education & Curriculum
Teacher · Language Assessor · Examiner · Developer  ·  ~14 years
▼

My career in education began in 2011 when I started the BA Health & Social Care — part-time, combined with work. From 2015 I worked full-time in education as a teacher, examiner and curriculum developer across multiple levels: secondary, special and vocational education. I taught Citizenship, Health & Social Care and English, developed school examinations and assessment policy, and guided digitalisation projects. What sets me apart is that I never just stood in front of a class — I always simultaneously improved the education around me, even when it was not asked of me. That pattern runs through every school I worked at.

Innovation & Research
Open innovation · STE(A)M · EU project · MSc · Conference paper  ·  ~10 years
▼

My interest in innovation started at Raldon Montage, where I analysed and improved processes and built project management software myself. It became scientific from 2015: at ArtIST I conducted qualitative research on open innovation in European higher education as part of my MSc thesis. Thesis: The Influence of Social Capital on the Creative Process of STE(A)M Education Realisation in Higher Education through Open Innovation within a European Context. This research resulted in: Kirkels, Y., Podmetina, D., Vlaming, L. (2026) The Art of Organizing Education Development within European Collaboration, Paper accepted at the R&D Management conference 2026. My conviction: creativity is the engine of real innovation — and I have demonstrated it.

Policy & Advisory
Policy advice · Change management · Quality assurance  ·  ~8 years
▼

My policy and advisory experience runs as a connecting thread through my entire career, even when the job title did not always reflect it. At Raldon Montage I was the director's strategic sparring partner for 17 years on investments, finance and business strategy. In education I advised school management on quality, digitalisation and innovation. At Esprit Scholen I held a formal role as policy officer for education and quality assurance. At the Dutch Education Union I chaired the Diversity & Inclusion Network for three years, providing policy advice on behalf of 81,000 members. My research on open innovation produced policy-relevant recommendations for European educational institutions.

Operations & HR
Finance · HR · Procurement · Software  ·  ~18 years
▼

At Raldon Montage I was responsible for the full range of business operations for 17 years: HR, finance, marketing, procurement and I built project planning and reporting software myself. I did not do this as someone following orders — I thought along with the company's direction and served as strategic sparring partner to the director. In 2018 I added an interim assignment as head of administration and project manager at Geert Groote College. I know the business side of organisations from the inside, and that makes me someone who can build bridges in education and the public sector that others cannot.

Digitalisation & AI
Azure AI · Scrum PSM I · e-learning · Quantum  ·  ~10 years
▼

My digital journey started at Raldon, where I built software for project planning and reporting myself — back in the nineties. In education I implemented Google for Education at Atlas College and developed digital learning environments and assessment systems. I hold certificates in Azure AI Fundamentals, Generative AI and Scrum Master. In 2025 I completed the National Quantum Course. I am not a technical developer, but someone who understands, implements and strategically deploys technology — and who knows the difference between technology as a goal and technology as a means.

Society & Connection
D&I · Democracy · Creativity · IDFA  ·  ~4 years
▼

My societal engagement is not a side note — it is my compass. I follow democracy, women's rights and equal opportunities closely, share my analyses on LinkedIn and ask the questions others leave aside. I chaired the Diversity & Inclusion Network at the Dutch Education Union for three years, representing 81,000 members. I served as a moderator at the International Documentary Festival Amsterdam. My BA in Cultural Management at HKU laid a foundation of creativity that I draw on every day. I believe creativity leads to innovation — and that connection between people, ideas and sectors is the basis of real progress.
