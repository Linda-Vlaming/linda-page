Who I am

The connecting thread

I build bridges between worlds that normally remain separate — business and education, practice and science, creativity and strategy. 35 years, and counting.

After 17 years in finance, HR, marketing and software development, I made a deliberate transition into education. As a teacher, examiner and curriculum developer I worked across secondary, special and vocational education. I conducted scientific research on open innovation in higher education (MSc, 2023) and contributed to a European STE(A)M project.


My engagement does not stop at my CV. I follow democracy, women's rights and equal opportunities closely, share my analyses and ask the questions others leave aside. I believe creativity is the engine of real innovation — and that good communication makes societal change possible.
